“teruggekeerd” is a collaboration with artist Sytse Vledder (Aching Hands).
The piece was directly inspired by our experiences with Christianity and the church, though from two completely opposing perspectives;
His perspective in this is piece is that of someone breaking free from the doctrine fed to him since childhood,
Reflecting on it with a sense of calm, peace and humor.
My own perspective was that of someone exploring a belief system of which was no trace in my youth.
The piece combines our perspectives and experiences, and, influenced by Joseph Campbell’s Monomyth,
follows a clear departure - initiation - return structure.
Hence the name for the piece; “teruggekeerd” (returned.)

Originally revealed in front of an audience at the 4DSound Venue at Contact Amsterdam, it has since been mixed to AmbiSonics format and stereo.
